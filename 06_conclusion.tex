\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Minimum $k$-instability witnesses distinguish a source-deletion budget $k$
from a required aggregate-answer change $\theta$. The optimization seeks
the smallest valid witness within that budget; existence is equivalent to
$\rho_\theta\le k$, where $\rho_\theta$ is the unbudgeted minimum size.
This separates stability at a prescribed budget from infeasibility for
every budget.

A fixed self-join query makes the budget decision problem NP-complete for
COUNT, nonnegative SUM, MAX, MIN, and AVG. The Vertex Cover construction
preserves the budget exactly and handles nonempty inputs for extrema and
averages through a permanent survivor. In contrast, singleton supports
yield polynomial-time exact algorithms for all five operators, with a
sorting-based algorithm for independent numeric rows.

These results identify source sharing, designated deletability, and
aggregate validity as central structural features of threshold stability.
Further work will develop the coverage and approximation analysis,
provenance-based execution methods, and a broader query classification.
